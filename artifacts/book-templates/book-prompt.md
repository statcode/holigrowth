## Output Format

Format the output as a fully structured book using markdown. Follow this formatting exactly:

- `# Chapter N: ...` for the 12 main chapters

- `---` for section breaks between major parts

## Pull-Quote Requirement (one per chapter)

**Every chapter must end with exactly one markdown blockquote** (a line beginning with `> `) that captures the single most resonant, evocative sentence from that chapter. This blockquote is rendered as a full-page featured pull quote in the printed book — a dedicated decorative page that sits at the end of the chapter and gives the reader a moment to breathe with one beautiful, memorable line.

The pull quote must:

- Be **one or two sentences** (1 sentence preferred, 2 max)
- Land between **120 and 280 characters total** — long enough to feel substantial, short enough to read in a breath
- **Stand alone** without context — a reader seeing this quote on its own should still feel something meaningful
- Be written in the **second person ("you")**, addressing the reader directly
- **Capture the emotional truth of the chapter**, not a summary of facts. Choose the line that sounds like wisdom whispered by an older self
- Avoid the reader's first name (the page already personalizes via header)
- Avoid astrology jargon unless it's poetic (e.g., "the moon teaches you patience" is fine; "your 6th house cusp in Aries" is not)
- Place it as the **last line of the chapter body**, AFTER any affirmations or sub-sections

Format the blockquote exactly like this — a single line, no nesting:

```
> The deepest intimacy you will ever experience is the one you build with yourself first.
```

If a chapter has affirmation sub-sections (Chapters 5, 6, 7) or mantra sub-sections (Chapter 10), the blockquote still goes at the very end of the chapter — after those sub-sections.
